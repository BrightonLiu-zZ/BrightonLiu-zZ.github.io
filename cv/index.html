Projects

  • 2026 - Now
    Self-Supervised Learning on Stellar Light Curves
    • First author, with Y. Ma and Prof. C. Theissen (UCSD). Paper accepted at ML4PS @ NeurIPS 2026.
    • Pre-trained a convolutional encoder with a GRU latent-prediction objective (PyTorch) on unlabelled TESS light curves; froze it and evaluated the embeddings with linear probes on 10 downstream tasks.
    • Concatenated the embeddings with 25 engineered features, improving linear-probe results on 7 of 10 tasks and beating a Conv1D and an MLP trained end-to-end; multi-seed experiments with bootstrap confidence intervals, tracked with Weights & Biases.
  • 2025 - Now
    KataGo × LLM — LLM Post-Training & Edge Deployment
    • Project lead of a volunteer team. Post-trained Qwen3-8B (LoRA, Hugging Face TRL, vLLM rollouts, Dockerized training) to play 9x9 Go and explain its moves by distilling the KataGo engine: GRPO first, then a rules-SFT / play-SFT / GRPO curriculum on 302k engine-verified examples; first wins vs a classical engine, zero illegal moves.
